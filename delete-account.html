Eagle Alpha 刪除帳戶說明

在 App 內刪除（建議）

  1. 開啟 Eagle Alpha 並登入。
  2. 進入「帳戶」頁面。
  3. 點選紅色的「刪除帳戶」。
  4. 輸入密碼確認，帳戶即被永久刪除。

會被刪除的資料

  • 你的登入帳戶（電子郵件與密碼）。
  • 雲端中的帳戶文件：用戶 ID、建立時間、訂閱／付費狀態與到期日。

自選股與價格提醒只存在你的手機上，解除安裝 App 即會清除。刪除後資料無法復原。

無法使用 App 時

請以註冊的電子郵件寄信至 fortunecloud.support@gmail.com，主旨寫「刪除 Eagle Alpha 帳戶」，我們會在 30 天內處理並回覆。

另見隱私權政策。

Eagle Alpha – Delete Your Account

Delete inside the App (recommended)

  1. Open Eagle Alpha and sign in.
  2. Go to the "Account" page.
  3. Tap the red "Delete Account" button.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. Your account is permanently deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password).
  • Your cloud account document: user ID, creation time, subscription/paid status and expiry date.

Your watchlist and price alerts exist only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the App. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the App

Email fortunecloud.support@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete Eagle Alpha account". We will process it within 30 days and reply.
