Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

Welcome to Eagle Eye Alpha. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Eagle Eye Alpha is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or location data is ever requested or stored by the app.

2. Data Usage & Local Storage

The app fetches real-time US stock market data from external APIs (such as Yahoo Finance and Finnhub). All user-specific settings, such as your Watchlist and encrypted API Keys, are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide real-time financial insights, the app interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Financial Data Providers: Yahoo Finance and Finnhub.io are used to fetch market quotes. These services may receive standard technical information (like your IP address) necessary to fulfill data requests.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and performance monitoring.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Secure Storage (Keychain/Keystore), to ensure that any API keys you provide are encrypted and safely stored on your device's hardware security module.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
